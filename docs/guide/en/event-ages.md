# How old?

How old were you when *Titanic* came out? And your child, your parents? This screen gives everyone's age on the date of each event.

## The ages at the top

- At first, only **your age** is shown.
- **Add an age** adds another person (15 to start with, from 1 to 100), younger or older than you. Up to **3 ages**, each with its own pictogram colour.
- **Tapping an age** changes it; **Remove** takes it away.
- These ages are the same as on [Equivalences](equivalences.md) and are **forgotten when the app closes**.
- Tapping **your** age replaces it for a while with another one ("Temporary ages"); **Back to my settings** brings back your real age.

## The list

- Without a search, every event, sorted by theme, from the most recent to the oldest.
- **My events**, the ones you added in [Settings](settings.md#my-events), come first, whatever themes are ticked; the search finds them too.
- The **Themes** button, above the search, ticks or unticks the families of events, as on [Equivalences](equivalences.md#themes): it is the same choice for both screens, and it is kept.
- The **Search for a film, a series, an artist…** field filters the titles from 2 letters on, ignoring accents and capitals.
- On each line, under the title, **the ages side by side, in the same order as at the top** and in each person's colour. Before a birth, the age is negative: "−14", that person was not born yet.
- Events less than a year old are not listed yet.

## Without a birth date

A message asks you to give it: **Open settings** takes you there, **Choose ages** lets you pick your age just this once. The list and the search still work, without the ages.
