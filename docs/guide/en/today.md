# Today

The opening screen: **today's reminder that time flies**.

## Today's sheet

At the top, the most striking anniversary of the day: the number of years in large type, the title of the event, its kind and date ("Cinema release · 2 November 2011"), and **You were 20.**

- **Round numbers** come first (10, 15, 20, 25 years…) and carry the **Round number** chip.
- For an event before your birth, your age is negative: "You were −3."
- **No anniversary today**: the sheet shows the first one of the next 7 days ("In 3 days") with "turns 25".
- The sheet takes the shape of the style chosen in [Settings › Display](settings.md#display): a **Polaroid** photo, a **VHS** tape or a **Tear-off calendar** page. The first time you open it each day, a short animation brings it in (the photo develops, the tape counter runs or yesterday's page is torn off), unless your device asks for fewer animations.

## Below

- **Also today**: the other anniversaries of the day.
- **Coming up…**: the anniversaries of the next 7 days, grouped by day ("Wednesday 4 November · in 2 days").

**Tapping the sheet or a card** opens [How old?](event-ages.md) on that event.

## New version

When a newer version is published, a **New version: TimeFlies X.Y.Z** banner appears at the top: **Download** opens its page, **Later** hides it until the next version. To stop looking for new versions, see [Settings › About](settings.md#about).
