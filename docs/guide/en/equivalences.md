# Equivalences

"For a 15-year-old, *Friends* is what *Happy Days* was for you." This screen finds the events **of the same type** that hold the same place in your life and in the life of someone younger or older: a cult sitcom for a cult sitcom, a console for a console.

## The ages at the top

The same as on [How old?](event-ages.md): your age, then **Add an age** for one to three other people, each with its own colour. Tapping an age changes it or takes it away (**Remove**). Under the ages, the gap with each person ("20 years apart").

- **With no age added**, the screen invites you to add one.
- **Small gap**: under 5 years, a window warns that the equivalences may not be representative. It is allowed all the same.
- The ages added are **forgotten when the app closes**.

## The cards

- One card per event of your life, from the most recent to the oldest, with its type at the top ("Death of a music icon").
- Side by side, in the order of the ages: **your event**, then **each person's equivalent**, with its date and how old they were. A person without an equivalent for that event gets a "—".
- From three columns on, the cards are more compact (short date, no kind).
- **Tapping a card** opens [How old?](event-ages.md) on your event.

## Themes

The **Themes** button ticks or unticks the families of events (Series & TV, Cinema, Music, Sport, Video games & tech, Politics & history), with **Check all** and **Uncheck all**. Your choice is kept.

## Without a birth date

**Open settings** to give it, or **Choose ages** to pick your age just this once.
