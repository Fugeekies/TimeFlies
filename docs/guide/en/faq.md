# Frequently asked questions

**Why only the birth month and year?**
It is less intrusive, and the day hardly changes the ages shown. Your age is counted from the 1st of the month.

**Is my data sent anywhere?**
No. The events are inside the app and your settings stay on your device. The only connection asks GitHub whether a new version exists, once a day at most, without sending anything else; it can be turned off in [Settings › About](settings.md#about).

**What does a negative age, like "−14", mean?**
That the person was not born yet: the event happened 14 years before their birth (started years included).

**There is no anniversary today.**
The sheet then shows the next one, within the next 7 days. The event base grows with each version.

**Why does a person get "—" on an Equivalences card?**
No event of the same type falls at the right time in their life, or it would fall in the future.

**I found a wrong date.**
Tap **Report a wrong date** in [Settings › About](settings.md#about): it opens the project's issue page on GitHub.

**The ages I added are gone.**
That is on purpose: they are forgotten when the app closes. Only your birth is saved.

**The widget did not update.**
Android sometimes delays night tasks to save battery. Open the app: the widget updates right away.

**How do I quit TimeFlies on Windows?**
Right click on its icon in the taskbar › **Quit**. Closing the window only hides it, see [the taskbar icon](windows-tray.md).

**Windows says it protected my PC during the installation.**
The installer is not signed by a known publisher. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**How do I get the dark theme?**
Polaroid and Tear-off calendar follow your device's theme; VHS is always dark. The style is chosen in [Settings › Display](settings.md#display).
