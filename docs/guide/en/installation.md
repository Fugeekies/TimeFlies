# Installation and first launch

## Install

Each version is published on the [Releases](https://github.com/Fugeekies/TimeFlies/releases) page, with one file per platform.

**Android** (8.0 and later)
1. On the phone, download `TimeFlies-X.Y.Z.apk` and open it.
2. Android asks you to allow installing apps from your browser: this is normal for an app that is not on the Play Store.

**Windows** (10 and 11, 64-bit)
1. Download `TimeFlies-X.Y.Z-Setup.exe` and run it. TimeFlies installs for your account, without administrator rights.
   > The installer is not signed by a known publisher: if Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## First launch

The **The saddest game in the world** screen opens only once:

- **Your birth month and year**: never the day, on purpose. They are used to work out your age at each event and stay on your device.
- **What interests you**: one chip per theme (Cinema, Music…) and per origin (France, United States…), all ticked. Untick what does not interest you; the details are set later in [Settings › Events](settings.md#events).
- **Let's go** saves everything and opens [Today](today.md).
- **Later** opens the app without a birth date: anniversaries are shown without your age. You can give it at any time in [Settings](settings.md).

## Update, uninstall

- **Update:** when a new version comes out, a **New version** banner appears on Today; **Download** opens its page. Install it over the old one: your settings are kept.
- **Uninstall:** like any app. On Android, your settings are deleted; on Windows (*Settings › Apps*), they stay in `%APPDATA%\TimeFlies` and a reinstall finds them again.
