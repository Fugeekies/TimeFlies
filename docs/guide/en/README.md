# User guide

TimeFlies makes you feel old: every day, the anniversaries of the films, series, songs, matches and big events that marked your life, and how old you were. The app runs on **Android** and **Windows**, with no account: your settings stay on your device.

## Getting started

- [Installation and first launch](installation.md)
- [Settings](settings.md)
- [Frequently asked questions](faq.md)

## The screens

The navigation bar (at the bottom on a phone, on the left on a PC) leads to the four screens, in this order:

| Screen | In short |
|---|---|
| [Today](today.md) | Today's reminder that time flies, and the anniversaries of the coming days |
| [How old?](event-ages.md) | How old you were, and up to 3 other people, on the date of each event |
| [Equivalences](equivalences.md) | The events that hold the same place in your life and in the life of someone younger or older |
| [Settings](settings.md) | Your birth, the style, the events shown |
