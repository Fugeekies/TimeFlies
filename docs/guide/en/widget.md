# The Android widget

Today's reminder on your home screen, the same as the sheet on [Today](today.md).

## Add it

In [Settings › Home screen](settings.md#home-screen-android), tap **Add the widget**. Otherwise: long press on the home screen › Widgets › TimeFlies.

## What it shows

It can be resized freely and picks the layout that fits:

| Size | Content |
|---|---|
| 2 × 2 | the number of years and the title |
| 4 × 2 | the date, the number of years, the title, its kind and year, your age |
| 4 × 3 and larger | the same, plus the next two anniversaries |

- It takes the colours of the chosen style (Polaroid, VHS or Tear-off calendar).
- **Tapping the widget** opens TimeFlies on Today.
- The **›** button, bottom right, moves to the next anniversary of the day, then back to the first. It only shows when there are several.
- Before the first launch, it shows "Open TimeFlies to get started."

## Updates

The widget changes **every day just after midnight**, when the phone starts and when you change a setting. It needs no connection. If Android delays the update to save battery, it updates at the latest when you open the app.
