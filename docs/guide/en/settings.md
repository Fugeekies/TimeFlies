# Settings

Every setting is saved as soon as it changes, with no button to confirm.

## Generations

**Your birth month and year**: never the day. **Clear** removes them: anniversaries are then shown without your age and the equivalences ask for an age. The other people's ages are not set here but on [How old?](event-ages.md) and [Equivalences](equivalences.md).

## Display

- **Style**: the style of the whole app and of the widget, with a preview of each.
  - **Polaroid** (default): a white-framed photo in the colour of the event's theme, ages written by hand.
  - **VHS**: a tape and its label, always dark.
  - **Tear-off calendar**: a calendar page, with the number of years as a big red figure.
  - Polaroid and Tear-off calendar follow the light or dark theme of your device.
- **Region**: France for now; it chooses the release dates used.
- **Language**: Français, English, Deutsch, Italiano or Español, each written in its own language. By default, your device's language when it is offered, otherwise French. It changes the whole app, the widget and, on Windows, the icon and the notification at once. Film and sports event titles follow the language too; songs and artists keep their names.

## My events

Your own dates: a wedding, a birth, a first flat… Their anniversaries show up on [Today](today.md), in the widget and the Windows notification, like those of the base, and you find them at the top of [How old?](event-ages.md). They have no equivalences.

- **Add an event**: a title (80 characters at most) and a date to the day, not after today.
- Touch an event to edit it or **Delete** it.
- **Export** saves your events to a `timeflies-events.json` file, wherever you choose (Drive, Downloads, a USB key…). **Import** reads that file on your new phone or PC: events already there (same title, same date) are not added twice, and nothing is erased.

These events stay on your device: nothing is sent over the Internet.

## Events

**Untick what does not interest you**: those events no longer appear anywhere, not on Today, not in the widget, not in the equivalences. The tree goes from theme to type, then to origin and sometimes to genre (for instance, keep French rap without US rap). Each line gives its number of events. Tap a box to tick or untick everything in it; **▸** expands a line.

## Home screen (Android)

**Add the widget** puts it on your home screen. Otherwise: long press on the home screen › Widgets › TimeFlies. See [the Android widget](widget.md).

## Windows

- **Start TimeFlies when Windows starts**: needed for the taskbar icon and the notification when the PC starts.
- **Notification when TimeFlies starts (once a day)**.
- **Keep TimeFlies in the taskbar when I close the window**.

See [the taskbar icon](windows-tray.md).
